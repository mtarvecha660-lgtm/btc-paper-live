How this was tested

    Weak spots

    • One coin, one exchange's prices, under 5 years out-of-sample. A few huge BTC moves drive every number.
    • These are famous ideas picked with hindsight (everyone knows trend filters worked on BTC). No test can remove that bias.
    • Trend and momentum trade rarely: they only just clear 30 trades (31 and 32), so treat their edge as thin evidence, not proof.
    • Real slippage in crashes can be worse than 0.05%. Indian tax (30% + 1% TDS) is not included.
    • BTC survived. The same rules on coins that died would look very different.
